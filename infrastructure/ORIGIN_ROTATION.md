# Future production origin separation

The owner confirms staging exposed **15.204.230.25** directly before Tunnel. Passive DNS, caches, scan history, certificate observations and third-party records may retain that association indefinitely. Changing a record to proxied mode or enabling Tunnel does not make this address secret.

Recommended choice A: after Tunnel is validated, provision a fresh production VPS/IP and never publish that IP in public DNS. Choice B: retain staging's existing IP, close direct API ports and explicitly accept that the historical origin remains discoverable. Neither hides the origin from Cloudflare, its hosting provider or administrators.
